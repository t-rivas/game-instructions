# Real image sources

This folder contains published product photography, official component images and credited public-reference images. No image in this folder was generated with AI.

- CATAN: CATAN Studio sixth-edition product pages and Cape Fear Games' card-accessory listing.
- Secret Hitler: Goat, Wolf & Cabbage product photography hosted by Dragon's Den Games and Ubuy.
- El Camarero: Maldón's official product gallery.
- Monopoly: Hasbro's official instructions page and a Hasbro product photograph hosted by Best Buy.
- Chess: Alan Light's `ChessSet.jpg` on Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 3.0), plus identification crops from Chess Villa India's Staunton product photograph.
- Burako: Ruibal's product gallery and a complete-set photograph hosted by ApioVerde.
- Uruguayan Truco: Mazo Yorugua product photography from Poppular Shop; the full Spanish deck and five card crops derive from Germarquezm and contributors' CC BY-SA 3.0 Wikimedia Commons image; the four-matas photograph is also from Wikimedia Commons.
- Skull King: current box, cards-in-play photograph and Expansion Pack product image from Grandpa Beck’s Games' official product pages.
- Sushi Go! and Sushi Go Party!: front and back tin images from Gamewright's official product pages.

Every displayed image has a bilingual caption and a clickable source URL in `new-games.js` or `more-games.js` and in the in-app image viewer.

The `*-box-cutout.png` files are deterministic transparent-background crops of the corresponding photographs above. They do not redraw or replace the packaging artwork and can be recreated with `scripts/make-cover-cutouts.ps1`.
