# Published artwork sources

These are original published game/card images, not AI recreations. Rights remain with the credited owners. This project is an independent personal companion guide. No ownership or permission to redistribute the publishers’ artwork is asserted.

## Editions and extraction

- Coup and Avalon use classic Indie Boards & Cards artwork. Role artwork reproduced on UltraBoardGames matches the printed classic cards; UltraBoardGames is the image host, not the publisher.
- Inquisitor is the complete front-cover illustration extracted from the publisher’s Reformation rulebook, hosted by Spelhuis. It is labeled as a box portrait, not a scan of the Inquisitor card.
- Polilla cards were extracted from Schmidt Spiele’s multilingual Mogel Motte rulebook. The box image is the German edition, explicitly labeled in the guide.
- Poker has no universal official deck. The Bicycle photo is a manufacturer product image hosted by distributor Asmodee. Interactive poker examples are code-drawn teaching diagrams.
- Images were resized/compressed, with transparent backgrounds flattened to white. The Coup, Avalon, Reformation and Dixit boxes are also provided as `.webp` cutouts with the white background removed, which the guide uses. Card art was not generated, repainted or translated. Printed labels remain in their source language.
- Rejected retailer images labeled “Inquisitor” and “allegiance mechanic” showed only base-game components and are not used.
